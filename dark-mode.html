About Me

I’m Sneha, a creative front-end developer with a passion for crafting responsive and interactive web experiences. I love combining design and technology to bring beautiful ideas to life in the browser.

I have hands-on experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and the Yii framework. I am also skilled in working with popular frontend libraries like Bootstrap and Tailwind CSS, helping me build clean, modern, and fully responsive websites.

Apart from my passion for coding, I am a proud sportsperson. I have represented the state of Kerala in Tennis and have been a State Champion for almost 10 years. Sports has taught me dedication, focus, and the spirit of never giving up — qualities I bring into my work as a developer too.

With a strong focus on performance, usability, and aesthetics, I enjoy creating websites that are not just functional but also beautifully designed. I'm always eager to learn new technologies and push the boundaries of what’s possible on the web.

Beyond coding, I am passionate about clean design, smooth animations, and crafting seamless user journeys. Let's build something amazing together!
